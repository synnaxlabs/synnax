## Why warmup and cool down matter

A dilution refrigerator keeps a quantum processor a fraction of a degree above absolute
zero. To change the processor, operators warm the fridge to room temperature, make the
change, and cool it back down. Each direction is a long procedure of pump, valve,
heater, and gas-handling steps, with pressure and temperature checks between them.

No experiments run on a fridge while it warms or cools. Each hour taken out of a cycle
is an hour of experiments given back.
