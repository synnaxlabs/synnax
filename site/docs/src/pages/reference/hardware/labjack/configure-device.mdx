The Driver connects to [LabJack](https://labjack.com/) T4 and T7 devices over USB,
Ethernet, or Wi-Fi. It finds them with the LabJack LJM library. Before a task can use a
device, you configure it. The T8 is not supported yet.

<Divider.Divider x />

## Prerequisites

<Platforms integration="labjack" />

- Install the
  [LJM library](https://support.labjack.com/docs/ljm-software-installer-downloads-t4-t7-t8-digit)
  on the computer that runs the Driver, then
  [restart the Driver](/reference/hardware/troubleshooting#restart-the-driver).
- Connect the device to that computer by USB, or to the same network.

<Divider.Divider x />

## How the Driver finds devices

The Driver looks for LabJack devices every few seconds. A USB device shows in the
[devices toolbar](/reference/hardware/devices#devices-toolbar) within a few seconds. A
device on the network can take about a minute.

- **Name** -> the model and the last four digits of the serial number, such as
  `LJM_dtT7-8422`.
- **Location** -> the connection, such as `LJM_ctUSB` or `LJM_ctETHERNET`.

The Console also shows the notification "New LJM_dtT7 connected", with a **Configure**
button.

## Configure a device

<Client.Tabs exclude={["cpp"]}>
  <Fragment slot="console">

1. In the devices toolbar, right-click the device and select **Configure**. You can also
   click **Configure** in the notification.
2. Enter a name, then click **Next**.
3. Enter an [identifier](/reference/hardware/devices#identifiers), then click **Save**.

<Video id="device-drivers/labjack/configure-device/lj-configure" />

  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="python">

Find the LabJack devices that the Driver found:

```python
for dev in client.devices.retrieve(makes=["LabJack"]):
    print(dev.key, dev.name, dev.model, dev.location)
```

The key of a LabJack device is its serial number. Configure devices in the Console.

  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="typescript">

Find the LabJack devices that the Driver found:

```typescript
for (const dev of await client.devices.retrieve({ makes: ["LabJack"] }))
  console.log(dev.key, dev.name, dev.model, dev.location);
```

The key of a LabJack device is its serial number. Configure devices in the Console.

  </Fragment>
</Client.Tabs>

Do not create LabJack devices in code. The Driver opens a device by its serial number,
so only a device that the Driver found works in a task.

## Troubleshooting

### The device does not show in the devices toolbar

Install the LJM library and
[restart the Driver](/reference/hardware/troubleshooting#restart-the-driver). LabJack
devices do not work on Linux. For more causes, see
[Troubleshoot hardware](/reference/hardware/troubleshooting#a-device-does-not-show-in-the-devices-toolbar).

### "A device matching the requested device parameters could not be found"

Another program holds the device. A USB device works with one program at a time. Close
[Kipling](https://support.labjack.com/docs/kipling) and other LabJack software, then
start the task again.

### "The device is temporarily unreachable. Will keep trying"

The Driver lost the connection to the device. Check the USB cable or the network. The
task continues when the device comes back.

For other problems, see [Troubleshoot hardware](/reference/hardware/troubleshooting).
