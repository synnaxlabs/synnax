The Synnax Console is where you set up hardware, see your data, and control your system.
It has a bar on the left, a bar at the top, and the mosaic in the middle.

## Parts of the Console

| Part             | Where                      | What it does                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project selector | Top of the left bar        | Shows the open [project](/reference/visualize/projects). Select it to open another.                 |
| Search           | Below the project selector | Opens the [command palette](#search-and-command-palette).                                           |
| Toolbars         | Middle of the left bar     | Open the [toolbars](#toolbars) for channels, ranges, devices, and more.                             |
| Component        | Bottom of the left bar     | Opens the [component toolbar](#component) for the selected plot or schematic.                       |
| Panel selector   | Left of the top bar        | Shows the panels of the project. A panel is one arrangement of tabs.                                |
| Help and updates | Right of the top bar       | Opens the docs (<Icon.QuestionMark />), and shows **Update available** when a new version is ready. |
| Mosaic           | Middle                     | Shows your line plots, schematics, tables, logs, and Arc editors in tabs.                           |

To split the mosaic, drag a tab to an edge of another tab.

## Toolbars

Select a toolbar icon in the left bar, or press its key, to open the toolbar.

| Toolbar                 | Key |
| ----------------------- | --- |
| Channels                | `C` |
| Ranges                  | `R` |
| Projects                | `W` |
| Devices                 | `D` |
| Tasks                   | `T` |
| Arcs                    | `A` |
| Statuses                | `S` |
| Users (Enterprise only) | `U` |
| Component               | `V` |

### <Icon.Channel />Channels

The [channels toolbar](/reference/channels#channels-in-the-console) lets you create,
view, and open the channels you want to visualize.

### <Icon.Range />Ranges

The [ranges toolbar](/reference/ranges/find) shows the ranges that you added to your
favorites. You can also create ranges and open the range explorer from it.

### <Icon.Project />Projects

The [projects toolbar](/reference/visualize/projects) lets you create, open, and export
projects.

### <Icon.Device />Devices

The [devices toolbar](/reference/hardware) shows the devices that Synnax is connected
to.

### <Icon.Task />Tasks

The [tasks toolbar](/reference/hardware/tasks) lets you create, configure, start, and
stop tasks.

### <Icon.Arc />Arcs

The [Arcs toolbar](/reference/control/arc/introduction) lets you create, start, and stop
Arc automations.

### <Icon.Status />Statuses

The [statuses toolbar](/reference/alarms) shows the statuses that you added to your
favorites. You can also create statuses and open the status explorer from it.

<Edition.Enterprise>

### <Icon.User />Users

The [users toolbar](/reference/enterprise/users) lets you view the users in your Synnax
deployment and change their roles.

</Edition.Enterprise>

### <Icon.Component />Component

The component toolbar is at the bottom of the left bar. It lets you edit the properties
of [line plots](/reference/visualize/line-plots), [logs](/reference/visualize/logs),
[schematics](/reference/visualize/schematics), and
[tables](/reference/visualize/tables).

## Search and command palette

{/* prettier-ignore-start */}

The search and command palette can be used to search for resources or to activate
commands.

- On Windows, press `Ctrl+P` to search for resources or `Ctrl+Shift+P` to search for
  commands.
- On macOS, press <code><Icon.Keyboard.Command />+P</code> to search for resources or
  <code><Icon.Keyboard.Command />+<Icon.Keyboard.Shift />+P</code> to search for
  commands.
