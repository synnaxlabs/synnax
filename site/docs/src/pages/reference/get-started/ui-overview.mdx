The Synnax Console is where you set up hardware, see your data, and control your system.
It has a bar on the left, a bar at the top, and the mosaic in the middle. Start the tour
below to walk through each part in order, or select any part to jump to it.

<ConsoleTour>

<Step step="mosaic">

The mosaic shows your line plots, schematics, tables, logs, and Arc editors in tabs. To
split the mosaic, drag a tab to an edge of another tab.

</Step>

<Step step="schematic">

A [schematic](/reference/visualize/schematics) shows your system and its live values.
Its valves and buttons send commands.

</Step>

<Step step="lineplot">

A [line plot](/reference/visualize/line-plots) plots channels over time, live as the
data arrives.

</Step>

<Step step="editor">

The [Arc editor](/reference/control/arc) shows the code of an Arc automation. The
controls at its bottom start and stop it.

</Step>

<Step step="project">

The project selector at the top of the left bar shows the open
[project](/reference/visualize/projects). Select it to open another project.

</Step>

<Step step="search">

{/* prettier-ignore-start */}

The search and command palette finds resources, such as channels and ranges, and runs
commands.

Press **Ctrl+P** (**Cmd+P** on macOS) to search for resources, or **Ctrl+Shift+P**
(**Cmd+Shift+P** on macOS) to search for commands.

{/* prettier-ignore-end */}

</Step>

<Step step="toolbars">

Select a toolbar icon in the left bar, or press its key, to open the toolbar.

<Edition.Enterprise>

The [users toolbar](/reference/enterprise/users) (**U**) lets you view the users in your
Synnax deployment and change their roles. Synnax Desktop does not show it.

</Edition.Enterprise>

</Step>

<Step step="channel">

The [channels toolbar](/reference/channels#channels-in-the-console) lets you create,
view, and open the channels you want to visualize.

</Step>

<Step step="range">

The [ranges toolbar](/reference/ranges/find) shows the ranges that you added to your
favorites. You can also create ranges and open the range explorer from it.

</Step>

<Step step="workspace">

The [projects toolbar](/reference/visualize/projects) lets you create, open, and export
projects.

</Step>

<Step step="device">

The [devices toolbar](/reference/hardware) shows the devices that Synnax is connected
to. You can also connect devices from it.

</Step>

<Step step="task">

The [tasks toolbar](/reference/hardware/tasks) lets you create, configure, start, and
stop tasks.

</Step>

<Step step="arc">

The [Arcs toolbar](/reference/control/arc) lets you create, start, and stop Arc
automations.

</Step>

<Step step="status">

The [statuses toolbar](/reference/alarms) shows the statuses that you added to your
favorites. You can also create statuses and open the status explorer from it.

</Step>

<Step step="component">

The component toolbar edits the properties of the selected
[line plot](/reference/visualize/line-plots), [log](/reference/visualize/logs),
[schematic](/reference/visualize/schematics), or [table](/reference/visualize/tables).

</Step>

<Step step="panels">

The panel selector on the left of the top bar shows the panels of the project. A panel
is one arrangement of tabs. To add and arrange panels, see
[Arrange panels and tabs](/reference/visualize/panels).

</Step>

<Step step="help">

The <Icon.QuestionMark /> icon on the right of the top bar opens the Synnax
documentation. When a new version is ready, the top bar shows **Update available**.

</Step>

</ConsoleTour>
