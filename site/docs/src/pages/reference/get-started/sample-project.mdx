The sample project is a simulated tank test in Synnax Desktop. A nitrogen supply fills
the tank through the press valve, and the vent valve empties it. An Arc automation
pressurizes the tank, holds it, and vents it. You watch the test on a schematic and a
line plot, take control of a valve, and abort the test. You need no hardware, and the
tutorial takes about five minutes.

You need [Synnax Desktop](/reference/get-started/install-desktop). The sample project is
not available in Synnax Enterprise.

## <Step step={1} level="h2">Open the sample project</Step>

In the list of projects, select **Sample project**. If a project is already open, run
the "Open sample project" command from the
[command palette](/reference/get-started/ui-overview#search-and-command-palette).

Synnax Desktop creates a project with:

| Item           | Kind                                         | What it does                                                                        |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tank           | [Schematic](/reference/visualize/schematics) | Shows the tank, its valves, and its pressure, with **Start** and **Abort** buttons. |
| Tank pressure  | [Line plot](/reference/visualize/line-plots) | Plots the tank pressure, and the state of each valve.                               |
| Press sequence | [Arc automation](/reference/control/arc)     | Runs the test: pressurize, hold, and vent.                                          |
| Tank simulator | Arc automation                               | Simulates the tank. It writes the pressure from the valve commands.                 |

Both Arc automations start at once. The tank pressure is 0 psi, and both valves are
closed.

## <Step step={2} level="h2">Run the test</Step>

1. On the Tank schematic, click **Start**. The schematic takes control of its valves and
   buttons, and sends the start command.
2. Click the control button (<Icon.Circle />) in the top-right corner of the schematic
   to release control. The Press sequence can now operate the valves.

The Press sequence opens the press valve, and the pressure climbs on the line plot. At
300 psi, the sequence closes the press valve and holds for 10 seconds. Then it opens the
vent valve until the pressure is below 5 psi, and waits for the next start.

## <Step step={3} level="h2">Take control of a valve</Step>

The schematic has a higher [control authority](/reference/control/authority) (200) than
the Press sequence (100). Thus an operator can take control from the sequence at any
time.

1. Click **Start**, and release control, as in step 2.
2. While the pressure climbs, click **Vent valve**. The schematic takes control, and the
   vent valve opens. The commands of the Press sequence to the valves now have no
   effect.
3. Click the control button (<Icon.Circle />) to release control. The Press sequence
   gets control of the valves again.

The control chip next to each valve shows which writer has control. Hover over a chip to
see a description. For more, see [manual control](/reference/control/manual).

## <Step step={4} level="h2">Abort the test</Step>

1. Click **Start**, and release control.
2. While the pressure climbs, click **Abort**.

The Press sequence closes the press valve and opens the vent valve. In the abort stage,
the sequence takes an authority of 255, which is absolute: no other writer can take
control of the valves. The sequence also aborts by itself when the pressure goes above
450 psi.

To run the test again after an abort, click **Start**, and release control.

## <Step step={5} level="h2">Read the Press sequence</Step>

Select the Press sequence tab at the bottom right to see its code. The sequence has five
[stages](/reference/control/arc/concepts/sequences-and-stages). It is in one stage at a
time, and it moves to the next stage when a condition is true.

| Stage        | What it does                                                           | Moves to                                                     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `idle`       | Closes both valves.                                                    | `pressurize` on **Start**.                                   |
| `pressurize` | Opens the press valve.                                                 | `hold` above 300 psi; `abort` above 450 psi or on **Abort**. |
| `hold`       | Closes the press valve.                                                | `vent` after 10 seconds; `abort` on **Abort**.               |
| `vent`       | Opens the vent valve.                                                  | `idle` below 5 psi.                                          |
| `abort`      | Closes the press valve, opens the vent valve, and takes authority 255. | `pressurize` on **Start**.                                   |

To learn to write a sequence like this one, see
[test sequences](/reference/control/arc/test-sequences).
