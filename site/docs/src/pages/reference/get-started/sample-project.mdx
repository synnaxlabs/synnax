The sample project is a simulated tank test. A nitrogen supply fills the tank through
the press valve, and the vent valve empties it. An Arc automation pressurizes the tank,
holds it, and vents it. You watch the test on a schematic and a line plot, take control
of a valve, and abort the test. You need no hardware, and the tutorial takes about five
minutes.

You need [Synnax Desktop](/reference/get-started/install-desktop).

<Divider.Divider x />

## <Step step={1} level="h2">Open the sample project</Step>

If the list of projects is empty, select **Open sample project**. If you already have
projects, open one and run the "Open sample project" command from the
[command palette](/reference/get-started/ui-overview#search-and-command-palette).

If the sample project already exists, the Console asks whether to reset it. A reset
removes your changes to the sample project and its Arc automations, so you can use it to
start again.

The Console creates a project with:

| Item           | Kind                                         | What it does                                                                        |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tank           | [Schematic](/reference/visualize/schematics) | Shows the tank, its valves, and its pressure, with **Start** and **Abort** buttons. |
| Tank pressure  | [Line plot](/reference/visualize/line-plots) | Plots the tank pressure, and the state of each valve.                               |
| Press sequence | [Arc automation](/reference/control/arc)     | Runs the test: pressurize, hold, and vent.                                          |
| Tank simulator | Arc automation                               | Simulates the tank. It writes the pressure from the valve commands.                 |

Both Arc automations start at once. The tank pressure is 0 psi, and both valves are
closed.

## <Step step={2} level="h2">Run the test</Step>

On the Tank schematic, click **Start**.

The Press sequence opens the press valve, and the pressure climbs on the line plot. At
300 psi, the sequence closes the press valve and holds for 10 seconds. Then it opens the
vent valve until the pressure is below 5 psi, and waits for the next start.

## <Step step={3} level="h2">Take control of a valve</Step>

The Press sequence has a higher [control authority](/reference/control/authority) (100)
than the Tank schematic (1), so a click on a valve has no effect while the sequence
runs. To take a valve from the sequence, click its control chip. The chip takes control
at 255, the highest authority.

1. Click the control chip next to **Vent valve**. The chip turns green.
2. Click **Vent valve**. The vent valve opens.
3. Click **Start**. The Press sequence opens the press valve, but its command to close
   the vent valve has no effect. The pressure stops near 140 psi, because the gas goes
   out through the vent valve as fast as it comes in.
4. Click **Vent valve** to close it. The pressure climbs again.
5. Click the green chip to give the vent valve back. The test continues as in step 2.

The control chip next to each valve shows which writer has control. Hover over a chip to
see a description. For more, see [manual control](/reference/control/manual).

## <Step step={4} level="h2">Abort the test</Step>

1. Click **Start**.
2. Before the hold ends, click **Abort**.

The Press sequence closes the press valve and opens the vent valve. In the abort stage,
the sequence raises its authority to 255, the same as a control chip, so its commands
also reach a valve that an operator holds. See
[virtual channels](/reference/control/authority#virtual-channels). The sequence also
aborts by itself when the pressure goes above 450 psi.

To run the test again after an abort, click **Start**.

## <Step step={5} level="h2">Read the Press sequence</Step>

The Press sequence tab on the right shows its code. The sequence has five
[stages](/reference/control/arc/execution-model#steps-of-a-sequence). It is in one stage
at a time, and it moves to the next stage when a condition is true.

| Stage        | What it does                                                           | Moves to                                                     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `idle`       | Closes both valves.                                                    | `pressurize` on **Start**.                                   |
| `pressurize` | Takes authority 100, and opens the press valve.                        | `hold` above 300 psi; `abort` above 450 psi or on **Abort**. |
| `hold`       | Closes the press valve.                                                | `vent` after 10 seconds; `abort` on **Abort**.               |
| `vent`       | Opens the vent valve.                                                  | `idle` below 5 psi.                                          |
| `abort`      | Closes the press valve, opens the vent valve, and takes authority 255. | `pressurize` on **Start**.                                   |

To learn to write a sequence like this one, see
[test sequences](/reference/control/arc/test-sequences).
