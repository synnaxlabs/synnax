Synnax comes in two editions. Synnax Desktop is one app that runs on your computer. It
has its own private database and its own Driver, so you connect hardware and see data
with nothing to set up. Synnax Enterprise is a Synnax Core that your team deploys on its
own hardware. Many people, scripts, and Drivers connect to it.

The two editions share most capabilities. The table shows where they differ.

## Choose an edition

Use Synnax Desktop when one person works on one computer. For example, an engineer at a
bench connects a LabJack or NI device, records data, and plots it. Synnax Desktop is
also a fast way to try Synnax.

Use Synnax Enterprise when a team shares one system. Choose Enterprise when you:

- Have more than one person who needs the same data.
- Connect hardware on other computers, such as a second PC or an NI CompactRIO.
- Write Python or TypeScript code that reads, writes, or controls data.
- Run Synnax on Linux or on a server.
- Need users, roles, and permissions.
